## 🔍 Two Pointers Patterns

### 1. Opposite Direction

Pointers start from both ends and move towards each other.

**Problems:**

* LC 11 — Container With Most Water
* LC 125 — Valid Palindrome
* LC 167 — Two Sum II
* LC 344 — Reverse String
* LC 977 — Squares of a Sorted Array

### 2. Fast & Slow Pointers

One pointer reads/scans while the other maintains the position for the required result.

**Problems:**

* LC 26 — Remove Duplicates
* LC 27 — Remove Element
* LC 80 — Remove Duplicates II
* LC 283 — Move Zeroes

### 3. Sorting + Two Pointers

Sorting helps reduce the search space and makes pointer movement predictable.

**Problems:**

* LC 15 — 3Sum
* LC 16 — 3Sum Closest
* LC 18 — 4Sum
* LC 881 — Boats to Save People

### 4. Partitioning / Multiple Pointers

Pointers divide the array into different sections according to conditions.

**Problem:**

* LC 75 — Sort Colors

### 5. Subsequence Matching

Pointers move through two strings while maintaining their relative order.

**Problem:**

* LC 392 — Is Subsequence
